# Thinking Clearly

The mind works best when it has room to breathe. In a world of constant notifications, infinite scrolls, and perpetual distraction, clarity becomes a practice rather than a default state.

## The Noise

We're surrounded by information, but information isn't the same as understanding. The challenge isn't finding more data—it's creating the conditions where genuine insight can emerge.

## Making Space

Some things that help:

- **Morning quiet** — The first hour sets the tone
- **Single-tasking** — Depth over breadth
- **Writing** — Thinking on paper reveals gaps in logic

> "The quieter you become, the more you can hear."

## A Simple Practice

Try this: before making any decision today, pause for three breaths. Notice what changes when you give yourself that small moment of space.

The goal isn't to think more. It's to think *better*.
